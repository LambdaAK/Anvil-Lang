How it reads

Your strokes are grouped into lines, words and letters: strokes that overlap from left to right make one letter (the dot of an i, the bar of a t); strokes that only touch might be one letter or two, and both are tried. Each letter is drawn again the way EMNIST's are (28×28, scaled to fill the frame) and the network gives a probability for each of 0–9, A–Z and a–z.

The network alone confuses l/1/I, o/0/O and s/5/S, and can't tell c from C. So each word is matched against a word list using those probabilities (a yellow reading below is one the word list corrected); a word that isn't in the list is kept as read, and a number as a number. Case comes from each letter's shape and its size on the line.
